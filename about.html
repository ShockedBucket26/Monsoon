About Monsoon

Monsoon Indian Bistro is an Indian restaurant located in Little Rock, Arkansas. We serve a variety of Indian dishes, from flavorful biryanis to classic butter chicken.

What's on the Menu?

Some favorites are Butter Chicken, Chicken Seekh Kabab, Paneer Tikka, Garlic Naan, and Biryanis.

We offer dine-in, online ordering, and catering.

Sunday Buffet

Our Sunday buffet runs from 11:00 AM to 3:00 PM.
